# Data Professionals Survey Dashboard (Power BI)

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard built using publicly available survey data collected from data professionals worldwide. The objective was to clean, analyse, and visualise the dataset to uncover trends relating to salaries, job satisfaction, career progression, and programming language preferences within the data industry.

The dataset was transformed and cleaned using Power BI's Power Query before being modelled into an interactive dashboard designed to communicate key findings in a clear and accessible format for stakeholders.

### Data Preparation

The dataset was cleaned and transformed in Power Query to improve consistency and support meaningful analysis. Key transformations included removing unnecessary columns, consolidating response categories, converting salary ranges into numerical values, and preparing the data for interactive reporting.

## Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes the following visualisations:

#### Survey Overview
- Total number of survey respondents.
- Average age of participants.

#### Average Salary by Current Role
- Compares average annual salaries across different data professions.
- Data Scientists reported the highest average salary (approximately $93k), while Data Developers reported the lowest average salary among employed respondents (approximately $33k).

#### Number of Survey Takers by Country
- Visualisation of the geographical distribution of respondents.
- The majority of participants were based in the United States.

#### Data Analysts' Avg Satisfaction with Salary
- Displays respondents' average satisfaction with their salary on a scale from 0 to 10.
- The average satisfaction score was 4.49, indicating that respondents were generally dissatisfied with their current salary.

#### Data Analysts' Avg Satisfaction with Work/Life Balance
- Shows respondents' average satisfaction with their work-life balance.
- The average rating was 6.0, suggesting moderately positive perceptions of work-life balance.

#### Average Salary of Data Analysts by Gender
- Compares average salaries between male and female respondents.
- Female respondents reported a slightly higher average salary (approximately $58k) compared with male respondents (approximately $54.3k).

#### Difficulty of Breaking Into Data for Career Switchers in the UK
- Illustrates how UK respondents who switched to a data career perceived the difficulty of breaking into the industry.
- "Difficult" was the most common response, followed by "Neither easy nor difficult."

#### Favourite Programming Language by Job Title
- Compares programming language preferences across different data-related occupations.
- Python emerged as the most popular programming language across nearly all job roles, including Data Analysts, Data Architects, Data Engineers, Data Scientists, Data Developers, and respondents who were students or seeking employment.

## Key Insights
- Data Scientists reported the highest average salaries among surveyed professionals.
- Most survey respondents were located in the United States.
- Data Analysts' overall salary satisfaction was relatively low despite moderate satisfaction with work-life balance.
- Female respondents reported a slightly higher average salary than male respondents for the Data Analyst role.
- Many UK respondents who switched careers perceived entering the data profession as difficult.
- Python was consistently the preferred programming language across the majority of data-related roles.
